Pitch

The residents of Fortune's Hollow, once united around the magic that made the town prosperous, have long since been isolated from each other by infighting and misfortune. For the treasure-hunting raccoon Finnegan, this is a chance to claim an unguarded bounty. But will his ambition survive his encounter with the desolate townsfolk and the noble minstrel who is intent on aiding them?

Work together with animal companions to utilize their unique abilities, and learn to play Harmonies that take on a magic of their own! Will you use them to help the dying town, or will you harness their power for your own purposes?

Description

Harmonicord is a non-linear 2D adventure full of puzzles, rope-swinging, and shenanigans. Players will explore the nooks and crannies of a quiet woodland while befriending forest creatures, playing songs, and solving conflicts.

The game features couch co-op, allowing a second player to take control of the NPC companions at any time and drop out whenever.

In this non-linear Metroidvania world, players will find themselves unlocking new abilities as they gain companions, allowing them to access areas of the map previously out of reach. They'll find that the goals they choose to pursue and the characters they choose to befried have an impact on the world and their experience, even determinig which of the multiple endings they arrive at.

This player freedom is reflected in the puzzle design, where sandbox-style challenges encourage players to discover their own solutions using the environment and the critters that accompany them.

The world is also littered with parkour challenges requiring the player to chain together rope-swinging action and traversal abilities provided by their companions.

Last but certainly not least, players will discover the titular Harmonies as they progress through the game and perform them via a Mandolin-playing minigame. They can even compose their own songs and perform them accompanied by the instruments of their companions!

A demo for Harmonicord is currently available on Steam and Itch.io!
